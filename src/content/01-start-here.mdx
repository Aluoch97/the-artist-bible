You don't need to learn the whole music business before your first release. You need to do a handful of things in the right order. This is that list.

**One note first:** the royalty sign-ups in this app are for **U.S. artists**. If you're based elsewhere, the steps are the same idea, but the organizations have different names. Look up the collecting societies in your own country.

## What this app is (and isn't)

It covers the business side: owning your songs, putting them out, and getting paid. It doesn't teach songwriting, recording or mixing.

## The path

1. **Finish the song and know who made it.** Everyone who helped write it, and who owns the recording, should be settled **before** the song goes anywhere. Use the [split sheet builder](/split-sheet) the same day you make the song.
2. **Understand what you own.** Every song is two things: the recording and the song underneath it. Read [Creation & Rights](/guides/creation-rights) (about five minutes).
3. **Choose how to release it.** Most independent artists use a *distributor*, a company that delivers your music to Spotify, Apple Music and the rest. Read [Distribution & Metadata](/guides/distribution-metadata) before you pick one, and check the current fees yourself.
4. **Plan the dates.** Put your release day into the [release planner](/release). It works backward and gives you a dated to-do list. Give yourself at least a few weeks.
5. **Set up your free royalty sign-ups.** Without them, money can sit unclaimed. The [royalty checklist](/audit) walks you through each one.
6. **Read anything before you sign it.** If someone offers you a deal, run it through the [contract check](/contract) and read the [red flags](/red-flags). Then ask an entertainment attorney about anything that matters.

## If you only have ten minutes

Do step 1 and step 5. A signed split sheet and your royalty sign-ups protect the most money for the least effort.

## Words you'll run into

Don't worry if terms like PRO, ISRC or metadata are new. Tap any underlined word in the app for a plain-English meaning, or browse them all under [Music words](/words).
