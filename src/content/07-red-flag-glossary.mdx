Most bad deals aren't hidden. They're written in language that makes you feel too unqualified to question it. This chapter turns that language into plain words.

## Five questions to ask about any deal

1. **What exactly am I giving up?** Ownership, money, time, or control?
2. **For how long?** "In perpetuity" means forever.
3. **What do I get, in writing?** Promises on a call don't count.
4. **How do I get out?** If there's no exit, there's no deal.
5. **Who benefits if I do nothing?** If the answer is "them," be careful.

## Rules of thumb

- **Real labels and real services invest their own money.** If you're paying a stranger for a "chance," be skeptical.
- **Pressure is a signal.** "Sign today" and "offer ends tonight" are sales tactics.
- **Never sign something you can't explain back** to a friend in one sentence.
- **Get a lawyer for anything involving ownership.** Many arts organizations offer low-cost help.
