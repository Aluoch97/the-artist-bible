# Beginner test: The Artist Bible

Goal: find out where a real beginner gets stuck. 3 to 5 people, 15 minutes each, on their **own phone**.

## Who to pick
Independent or aspiring artists who have NOT used the app. Not friends who will be polite about it. Ideally: someone with a song out or nearly out, someone who has never released anything, and someone who has been handed a contract.

## How to run it
1. Send them the link (bible.nrnszn.com). Say nothing about how it works.
2. Sit with them or screen-share. Ask them to **think out loud**.
3. Do not help. If they are stuck for more than 30 seconds, write down where, then give a hint.
4. Give the three tasks below, one at a time.

## Tasks
1. "You are releasing a song on a date in about two months. Find out what you need to do and when."
2. "Your song is on Spotify. Check whether you are missing any money from it."
3. "You and a producer made a song together. Write down who owns what and send it to them."

Optional 4: "Someone sent you this contract. Is it okay to sign?" (use the example contract in the app).

## Write down, for each task
- Did they finish? How long did it take?
- Where did they hesitate, tap the wrong thing, or say "what does that mean?"
- Any word they did not understand?
- Did they trust what the app told them? Why or why not?

## Ask at the end
1. What was the most useful thing? The most confusing?
2. Would you use this again? For what?
3. What did you expect to find that wasn't there?
4. On a scale of 1-5, how clear was it? (1 = lost, 5 = obvious)
5. Would you tell another artist about it?

## What to do with the results
Anything two or more people stumbled on is a real problem: fix it. Anything only one person raised is a note. Send the findings back and we will turn them into changes.
