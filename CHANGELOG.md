# What's new in The Artist Bible

Short version numbers: 1.0, 1.1, 1.2 ... A small change goes up by one step; a big redesign starts a new first number.
The newest version is on top, and it is the number shown in /admin (Account). Write each entry in plain words.
To release: add a new `## x.y · date` block at the top of this file in the same change. The build picks it up by itself.

## 1.10 · Nov 26, 2026
Fixed: **Download PDF** on the Release page. The old Print / Save PDF button did nothing on phones and in the installed app. It now saves a real PDF of your plan (every step with its date, ticked boxes for the ones you have done, and "Behind" on late steps), the same way the split sheet and press kit already do.

## 1.9 · Nov 26, 2026
Fixed: on a computer, the little calendar icon in every date box sat on top of the "Tap to choose a date" hint. It now sits at the right edge of the box.

## 1.8 · Nov 26, 2026
Settings. The sun/moon button at the top is now a **Settings** (gear) button.
- Pick light, dark or match my device, and make the text smaller, larger or largest.
- Higher contrast, underlined links, a plainer background (no frosted glass or glow) and reduce motion.
- Reset to defaults. Choices are saved on your device and applied before the page shows, so there is no flash.

## 1.7 · Nov 26, 2026
A more immersive Tools tab. A colourful banner, tools grouped by what you are trying to do (Protect yourself, Keep track, Know your numbers, Get noticed), bright tiles like the Home cards, and small badges that show your own progress (songs, shows, budget).

## 1.6 · Nov 26, 2026
The BMI link on the Live show log page and in the Royalties guide now opens the BMI home page. The old BMI Live address was a dead end.

## 1.5 · Nov 26, 2026
Fixes from iPhone screenshots.
- **Release date field:** the date text now sits in the middle of the box instead of at the top edge.
- **Your timeline** shows every step at once, in two short rows, instead of sliding sideways and cutting off the last steps.
- **Less empty space** at the bottom of every page, above the footer.

## 1.4 · Nov 26, 2026
A way to report a security problem is now published at /.well-known/security.txt. Nothing looks different in the app.

## 1.3 · Nov 26, 2026
- **Examples for every music word.** Tap any underlined word, or open Learn > Words, and each one now has a short real-life example under its meaning.

## 1.2 · Nov 26, 2026
Tidier and faster.
- **Each thing lives in one place.** The Red flags and Get Paid pages are no longer repeated inside the guides (the guides link to them instead), the Marketing guide links to the Release planner instead of listing the steps again, and Home no longer repeats your progress below the three cards.
- **Contract check lives under Tools** (its own page with a back link). Learn now has three tabs: Guides, Red flags and Words, so they fit on one line.
- **Faster to open.** Each page loads only when you open it, so the first load is about half the size. Everything is still saved for offline use.
- **Fixes:** the About page, search and share descriptions and install shortcuts list the current tools; plain wording on Home; matching dates on Privacy and Terms; the Words tab now highlights Learn; Release and Split sheet no longer scroll sideways at very large text sizes.
- **Safer:** the site no longer allows inline styles, and the security header now covers subdomains.

## 1.1 · Nov 25, 2026
- **Tap to update**: when a new version is ready, a small bar appears at the bottom. Tap it to get the new version. Your saved work is not touched.
- **Admin page** (/admin) for the owner: password sign-in, setup checklist, today's AI-review usage, content freshness and the app version.

## 1.0 · Nov 25, 2026
The first full release: Release planner, Get Paid checklist, Tools (split sheet, streaming earnings, My songs, live show log, press kit and pitch, release budget, contract check), seven guides, Red flags and Music words, light and dark themes, and an offline app you can install.
